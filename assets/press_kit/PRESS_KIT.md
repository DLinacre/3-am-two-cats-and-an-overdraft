### 🎙️ Artist Biography

**David Linacre** is a British multi-instrumentalist, producer, and audio engineer based in the UK. Operating at the intersection of analog warmth and cutting-edge neural music synthesis, Linacre produces intimate, narrative-driven Lo-Fi hip-hop, neo-soul, and chillhop. 

Known for his unhurried 78 BPM boom-bap swing, dusty vintage Fender Rhodes voicings, tape-saturated upright bass, and self-deprecating yet soulful lyricism, David transforms humble bedroom realities into cinematic, broadcast-grade audio experiences.

---

### 💿 The Album Concept

*“This album is dedicated to anyone who ever poured their last thirty pounds of overdraft into audio gear instead of groceries, while their pets looked at them like they were completely insane.”* — **David Linacre**

Recorded entirely in the quiet hours between 1:00 AM and 6:00 AM, *“3 AM, Two Cats & An Overdraft”* is a concept piece exploring the stubborn persistence required to create art. Across 16 tracks, the album documents the humorous battles with demanding feline companions (who frequently step on DAW transport controls), bounced emails to unresponsive record executives, the micro-economics of streaming royalties, and the profound beauty of midnight inspiration.

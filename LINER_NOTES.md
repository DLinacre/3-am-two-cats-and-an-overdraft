## 📖 The Story Behind the Album

Recorded in the small hours of the night in a humble home studio, **"3 AM, Two Cats & An Overdraft"** is David Linacre's definitive musical statement. Armed with only an audio interface held together by electrical tape, an empty bank account, two demanding feline companions serving as executive producers, and an RTX 3070 Ti running local neural synthesis, David crafted 16 tracks of pure, unfiltered, soulful chillhop.

Every snare was swung by ear. Every Rhodes voicing was tuned to soothe tired eyes. Every lyric reflects the hilarious, bittersweet, and stubborn beauty of refusing to give up on creating art.

## 🎧 About the Album

**"3 AM, Two Cats & An Overdraft"** is a 16-track concept album chronicling the gritty, hilarious, and deeply soulful reality of independent bedroom music production: an audio interface held together by electrical tape, an overdraft warning on the banking app, two feline landlords demanding gourmet salmon on the spacebar, and an unstoppable passion for golden-era storytelling hip-hop and late-night neo-soul.

Self-produced by **David Linacre**, synthesized locally with neural audio diffusion, and mastered to broadcast EBU R128 standards.

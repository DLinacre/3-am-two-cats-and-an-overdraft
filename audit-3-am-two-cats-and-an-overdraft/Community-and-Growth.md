# Community Growth, Audience Building & Creative Distribution
## Project: *David Linacre - 3 AM, Two Cats & An Overdraft* (2026)

---

## 1. Target Audience Segments

1. **The Bedroom Producer Community:**
   Artists wrestling with DAWs, budget audio interfaces, VST latency, and financial constraints who see their own lives mirrored in the lyrics.
2. **Lo-Fi Chillhop & Study Beats Listeners:**
   Students, programmers, and night owls who want warm, comforting, dust-crackling music with soulful storytelling.
3. **Cat Lovers & Domestic Creatives:**
   Pet owners charmed by Tabby stepping on the spacebar and Tuxedo supervising mix sessions.

---

## 2. Growth Channels

- **Reddit:** Communities like `r/LofiHipHop`, `r/WeAreTheMusicMakers`, and `r/cats`.
- **YouTube:** 24/7 Lo-Fi Chillhop Live Stream looping the 16 broadcast visualizer videos.
- **TikTok / Instagram Reels:** Short 15-second clips contrasting the high-energy rap of *Overdraft Love Letter* with the sleepy purring of *Two Little Purrs in the Monitor Mix*.

# Artificial Intelligence, Ethics & Autonomous Music Workflows
## Project: *David Linacre - 3 AM, Two Cats & An Overdraft* (2026)

---

## 1. Ethical Transparency & Artistic Intent

This album represents a pioneering fusion of human lyrical storytelling and neural audio diffusion:
- **Human Author:** All concepts, themes, rhymes, musical structure, chord plans, and emotional direction written by **David Linacre**.
- **Neural Synthesis:** Audio synthesized via local diffusion model (MiniMax Music 3 DiT) running on an NVIDIA RTX 3070 Ti.
- **Mastering & Rendering:** Automated via custom FFmpeg DSP pipelines adhering to standard broadcast loudness curves (EBU R128).

---

## 2. Innovative Future AI Features

1. **Interactive "Studio Companion" Tabby & Tuxedo Bot:**
   An on-site conversational agent embodying the sarcastic, sleepy personalities of the two cats that comments on the tracks and offers humorous advice to broke producers.
2. **Dynamic Lyric Timestamps via Whisper AI:**
   Utilize OpenAI Whisper locally to automatically generate word-level WebVTT subtitles for seamless synchronized lyric sing-alongs.

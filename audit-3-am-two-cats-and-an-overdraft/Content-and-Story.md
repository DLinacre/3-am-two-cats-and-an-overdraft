## Project: *David Linacre - 3 AM, Two Cats & An Overdraft* (2026)

## 1. Narrative Architecture Across the 16 Tracks

The tracklist follows a classic 3-act narrative arc:

### Act I: The Midnight Onset (Tracks 1–5)
- **Track 01:** *Two Cats, Zero Pounds (Genesis at 3 AM)* — The world sleeps; David sits with his cats, rain on the glass, setting up the session.
- **Track 02:** *Feline Landlords on the Spacebar* — The comical intrusion of the pets walking across the keys and demanding dinner.
- **Track 03:** *Overdraft Love Letter* — The reality check: high-energy boom-bap rap directly responding to the banking app's red notifications.
- **Track 04:** *Coffee Stains & Broken Preamps* — The DIY gear ethos: analog tape holding hardware together.
- **Track 05:** *Sent to an A&R's Ghost* — Sending demos into the void of the modern music industry without a response.

### Act II: The Grind & The Flow (Tracks 6–11)
- **Track 06:** *Tuna Can Grammys* — Stacking empty pet food cans into trophies while mixing drum loops.
- **Track 07:** *Four Chords, Nine Lives* — Stripping away commercial complexity for pure emotive progression.
- **Track 08:** *The Space Between Paychecks (Interlude)* — An acoustic ambient breath of vulnerability.
- **Track 09:** *Bedroom Platinum* — Redefining success: it's not a wall plaque; it's completing the master reel.
- **Track 10:** *Cat Hair on the Pop Filter* — Laughing at studio imperfections while laying down vocals.
- **Track 11:** *Streaming for Pennies* — The economics of fractions of a cent per spin.

### Act III: The Epiphany & The Dawn (Tracks 12–16)
- **Track 12:** *4 AM Epiphany* — When exhaustion turns into pure melodic breakthrough.
- **Track 13:** *Whispering in the Mic (Don't Wake the Neighbors)* — Delivering hushed vocal warmth in a paper-thin apartment.
- **Track 14:** *Bounced to MP3* — Finalizing the stems and hitting render.
- **Track 15:** *Two Little Purrs in the Monitor Mix* — The cats curling up as the mix balance settles.
- **Track 16:** *Sunrise on the Interface (Outro)* — Daylight breaks through the blinds; session complete.

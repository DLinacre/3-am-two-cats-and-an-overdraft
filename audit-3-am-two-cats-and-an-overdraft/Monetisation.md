# Commercial Potential, Merchandising & Monetisation Strategy
## Project: *David Linacre - 3 AM, Two Cats & An Overdraft* (2026)

---

## 1. Commercial Roadmap

```
[Phase 1: Free Stream] ──> [Phase 2: Digital Deluxe] ──> [Phase 3: Physicals & Stems]
GitHub Pages & YouTube      Bandcamp 24-Bit FLAC + PDF     Limited Cassette Tape Run
Free Interactive Player      Bonus Instrumental Stems      "Two Cats" Sample Pack
```

### Monetisation Vehicles
1. **Bandcamp "Name Your Price" Deluxe Digital Package:**
   Includes uncompressed 24-bit 48kHz FLAC master audio files, full digital lyric booklet PDF, and isolated vocal/instrumental stems.
2. **Limited Edition Physical Cassette Tapes:**
   Lo-fi hip-hop has a thriving cassette collector community. A run of 100 amber-tinted cassette shells with dual J-card artwork.
3. **The "Two Cats" Producer Sample Pack:**
   Packaged royalty-free Rhodes loops, dusty vinyl noise, vintage drum one-shots, and authentic cat purr ambient layers.
